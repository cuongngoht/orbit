# How to extend

One row per kind of change: what to touch, and the test that proves it. Read the row for your change BEFORE you
write it -- a change made outside its row (a list of verbs in a caller, a product word in `src/`, a hand-edited
generated schema) is the kind the enforced rules in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) exist to refuse.
