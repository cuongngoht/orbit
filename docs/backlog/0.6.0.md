**Status:** re-planned 2026-11-12 on `release/0.6.0` (`fe1dca8`, nine commits past `v0.5.0` = `54aaf0b`). Drafted
first on 2026-11-08 while the 0.5.0 ship ran (Q1–Q9 below); the owner then narrowed 0.6.0 to three pillars and asked
for the in-repo benchmark to be replaced by an evaluator that is independent of the product.

**Built (2026-11-12).** Every item below is built and merged on the release branch; each says what it does not prove.
The evaluation of the release is the lead's, in `evaluator/results/0.6.0/REPORT.md`.

**Owner decisions (2026-11-12).**

1. A release qualifies on the fast tier (`ship.mjs --ci`) plus a record of the independent evaluator for that exact
   artifact. `benchmark/` and the ship's benchmark machine (ADR 0049, 0051) are removed once the evaluator works.
2. Q2 is done in 0.6.0 with Q3, Q7 and Q8. Q4, Q5, Q6 and Q9 are defects of the old benchmark: they end with it, and
   their lessons are built into the evaluator (a cleanup never replaces the error; no run with no verdict counts;
   scratch trees are dropped; a leak check on what an agent is handed).
3. Task source: SWE-bench Verified (revision `c104f840cc67f8b6eec6f759ebc8b2693d585d4a`) plus local packs converted from
   the old fixtures. Real model runs: about $50 for the Claude Code arms, Sonnet 5.5 at medium effort; about $15 more
   for the two mini-SWE-agent arms once a key of their own is supplied (`ORBIT_EVAL_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`).

**Rules for this list.** Every fix is the most general one (name the principle, derive it from the one place that
decides it, hold it with a test across every existing design, control and fixture). Never loosen an acceptance rule,
a truth, a verifier or a gate. One item to one branch, merged one at a time, the full suite run alone after each.

## The seven features

| # | Feature | Items |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three sufficiency levels: enough to investigate, to change, to conclude | U1 |
| 2 | File-level relations followed on demand; a missing language adapter is a gap, never a guessed edge | U3 |
| 3 | A bounded discovery loop on the task's own work, reused by the next session | U2 |
| 4 | Advice from evidence: one line naming the right skill or agent; silence on a simple change | P1, P2, P5 |
| 5 | A turn is not left unverified: edits with no check after them are held once, naming the check | P3 |
| 6 | An independent evaluator: plain host against Orbit 0.5.0 and 0.6.0, graded by hidden tests | E1–E4 |
| 7 | A release gate on the fast tier and the evaluator's record | R1–R4 |

## Gap analysis (audit of `fe1dca8`)

**Already works, reused as it is.**
- `context --for` composes the owners memory-first and answers RELEVANT (items classed direct, dependency, downstream,
  verification or unknown, each with its evidence and freshness) and one SUFFICIENCY word, ENOUGH, DISCOVER or ASK,
  with targeted reads (`src/context/`, `cli/context-grounding.mjs`). A host's references are verified against the tree.
- Freshness is keyed to each record's own scope fingerprint, never HEAD (`src/freshness.mjs`).
- Pre-change reach and owed checks (`src/orchestration/impact.mjs`, `obligations.mjs planOf`).
- The work journal: needs to prove, to find out and to do; settlement with scope fingerprints; checkpoints; resume and
  recall across sessions (`src/work/`).
- Prompt-time strategy NONE, DIRECT, COORDINATED, SATELLITE, RESUME (`src/routing/strategy.mjs`); the Stop hold for a
  session's own workflow (`src/lifecycle/subscribers/hold.mjs`); the activation fold (`src/trace/projections/`).
- Inside an engineering task: failure classes from demonstrable facts, failure signatures, NO_PROGRESS, budgets,
  reconcile without resend, the completion rows (`src/orchestration/`, `src/automation/`).

**Exists, not yet connected.**
- SUFFICIENCY answers only "enough to choose a scope"; nothing says whether a change can be made or a task concluded.
- The work journal is never read by `context` as the record of a task's understanding, and nothing bounds rounds.
- Failure classification exists only inside a task: a plain session's failed test gets no diagnosis line.
- Impact is asked only inside a task's plan; the Stop hold only for a coordinated workflow.
- A decision's reason lives in the task journal, and nothing joins it to the receipts that allowed the next step.

**Missing.**
- Three levels of sufficiency; a bounded loop across rounds; relations finer than a module (import edges are
  module to module, from one regex); a gap for a language no adapter reads.
- Conditions of use: catalog rows declare keywords, never the facts that should bring a skill or agent in.
- Advice from evidence after the prompt (a failed or repeating check, an open knowledge need, edits left unverified).
- A research flow that records sourced, version-scoped knowledge.
- A split of what stopped a check into environment, dependency, access and product.
- A plain-host baseline on any real engineering task: the benchmark measured Orbit against Orbit's own cases.

## Items

### E — Independent evaluation (`evaluator/`, a package of its own)

- **E1 Core.** Its own `package.json`, checks and results; imports nothing of the product and is imported by nothing;
  export-ignored; splittable with `git subtree split --prefix=evaluator`. One fresh container per arm, task and trial;
  the pinned Claude Code; network only to the model API through an egress proxy; identical model, effort, tools,
  limits and prompt across arms; a fresh store and home per run. Outcome words from what the grader and controller
  observed: RESOLVED, UNRESOLVED, REGRESSED, EMPTY_PATCH, FALSE_DONE, TIMEOUT, BUDGET, INFRA_ERROR, UNGRADABLE. A hard
  budget ledger; a journal a run resumes from; a credential scan of every room.
  *Status:* built and merged; self-checks held without a model (packs 19/19, SWE-bench 5/5, mini 4/4); resume proven on a killed campaign.
- **E2 Tasks and graders.** SWE-bench Verified rows pinned by revision and row digest, graded by the official
  `swebench` harness on the extracted patch; local packs from `orders` and `lending` graded by hidden node tests in a
  network-less container. The agent never holds a grading test or a gold patch, and the plugin archive it is handed
  holds no part of the evaluator.
  *Status:* built and merged: ten packs, SWE-bench Verified on `swebench` 4.1.0 (5.x cannot read these rows).
- **E3 Arms.** Claude Code plain, + Orbit `v0.5.0`, + Orbit 0.6.0; mini-SWE-agent plain and + Orbit (the skill listing
  in its system prompt, Orbit's CLIs through its shell; it measures understanding only, since it has no hooks).
  *Status:* built and merged. The mini-SWE-agent arms ran their self-checks only: their real runs need `ORBIT_EVAL_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and are NOT_RUN without it.
- **E4 Pre-registration and evidence.** `evaluator/prereg/0.6.0.json` committed before any 0.6.0 behaviour change:
  revision, seeded selection, dev and held-out sets, arms, limits, prompt, scoring and the verdict rule IMPROVED,
  NO_DIFFERENCE, REGRESSED or INCONCLUSIVE. Self-controls without a model: gold, no-op that says done, half fix, a patch
  that edits tests, killed container, over time. Results and provenance of the old benchmark under `evaluator/`.
  *Status:* committed before any model run (`3b48239` fixed the counts after the pilot: dev 3 + 2, held-out 4 + 2); pilot RESOLVED; results in `evaluator/results/0.6.0/`.

### U — Project Understanding (extends `context --for` and the work journal; no new owner, no new room)

- **U1 Three levels.** `sufficiencyOf` also answers `levels.{investigate,change,conclude}`, each from an ordered table
  over demonstrable facts, never from a count of hits.
  *Status:* built and merged (ADR 0058).
- **U2 Bounded rounds.** Done criteria are proof needs, gaps are knowledge needs, a round is a checkpoint. A pure fold
  bounds rounds, stops after two rounds that settle nothing new, and never offers a target again while what answered
  it is settled and fresh. A later session re-reads only what went stale.
  *Status:* built and merged (`src/work/rounds.mjs`, `context --work=`, `work checkpoint --asked=`).
- **U3 File-level relations on demand.** One hop out and one hop in from the direct paths, through a relation adapter
  contract in `src/context/`; languages live in a pack under `skills/`. No adapter: a gap with the host's way to fill
  it. A route-shaped literal shared across files is a hypothesis until the host verifies it.
  *Status:* built and merged, multi-line imports included. Not done: architecture's own per-line import scan, Python re-exports through `__init__`.
- **U4 Freshness events.** A test for edit, rename, delete and branch switch across every owner that keeps facts;
  the smallest fix each event demands.
  *Status:* built and merged: a scope gone is GONE, a need settled on it invalidated.
- **U5 Research.** An instruction-only `orbit-research` skill over existing doors: the technology and version from the
  manifests, a knowledge need scoped to the manifest, the provider's answer or the host's sourced lookup, a claim with
  that version as its context. A version bump makes it stale; a repeated question is the same need.
  *Status:* built and merged (`orbit-research`). A host's own lookup settles the need but records no claim.

### P — Proactive Execution (the host reasons; the engine bounds and keeps the evidence)

- **P1 Conditions of use.** `reactsTo` on catalog rows, over a closed vocabulary of facts the engine can derive; a test
  holds words, derivers and rows to each other.
  *Status:* built and merged: `check.failed`, `check.repeats`, `knowledge.owed`. Dropped: `verification.owed` (P3 holds it), `change.spans-components` (no playbook handles it).
- **P2 Advice from evidence.** At most one line after a failure or at the next prompt, naming the fact, the component
  and the command; an ordered silence table (halt cue, paused or waiting work, said already, a cap per prompt).
  *Status:* built and merged (ADR 0059). Hook cost measured, about 40-50 ms router and 55 ms per failed call.
- **P3 Verified before stopping.** Edits under a component with a declared safe check and no passing check after them:
  the turn is held once, naming the check (`trigger.holdUnverified`, set only in Orbit Control).
  *Status:* built and merged. Known: a check whose command does not classify as test, lint or build never clears the cheap row; `unverified` is session-wide.
- **P4 What stopped a check.** DEPENDENCY and ACCESS beside ENVIRONMENT, from demonstrable facts only.
  *Status:* built and merged; proven on the module and fakes, not end to end through the CLI.
- **P5 No loops.** The same command failing again with no edit between is said once as no progress; every owner with a
  write door names its read-back.
  *Status:* built and merged.
- **P6 Explainable.** `advice.given` on the trail, followed by the activation fold; `task why <task>`.
  *Status:* built and merged (`advice.given`, `task why`).
- **P7 Q3 and Q7.** No line a host sees carries a placeholder; every resume target names its command.

### Q1. What the engine requires is shown before the host designs (branch `qa-accepted`, built: `5804b63`)

**Built, parked, not merged:** branch `qa-accepted` (4 commits on `2e4b362`): each obligation's template carries a
`ruling` computed by the mapping stage's own compiler; every refusal that requires a criterion names its candidate
(only the obligation's own criterion) or says none exists; a page design that ends with no report is refused at
`rules` by name (`ENDS_REPORT`), since generation cannot add a report only the design knows. Property tests over the
worked and reference designs and their mutations (`test/qa-actionable.mjs`), fixtures in `test/fixtures/qa-refusals/`.
Focused tests 423/0 and 26/0, strict lint clean; no full suite yet. Its CHANGELOG line sits under 0.5.0 and moves to
0.6.0 at merge. Before 0.6.0 ships: all seven ORBIT-QA rows (and TOOL-001) on Sonnet at medium, since every QA host's
context and refusal texts change. Left: the contract is per kind of step, not per bound adapter; the property is held
over the corpus, not arbitrary designs.

ORBIT-QA-001 missed its planted defect (persisted-after-write) in two of three tries of the 0.5.0 ship
(`bnr-2fc9adb2480f`, `bnr-0bb060ca0431`; held on the third, `bnr-84624a2cb8b9`): every design was refused and the
obligation's design budget was spent.

- The trigger step must be ruled by an *accepted* criterion (`src/qa/generation/compile.mjs` `structuralTree`), but the
  obligation's template showed only `expected: [UI-AC-2/E1]`, and the refusal named no criterion. The one that rules it,
  `UI-AC-2/E3`, was in the plan; a passing run found it by luck.
- A page design passed every generation stage, and the case the ENGINE generated from it was refused by the validator
  ("does not end with a report step"): what generation made, validation did not accept.

Principles: (1) the design contract -- per step role, the criteria that can rule it and the template each compiles to --
is computed by the functions that refuse and shown in the obligation's template; (2) every generation refusal names the
way out (its candidates, or that none exists and what holds instead), held by a test over every stage; (3) what
generation makes, the validator accepts, held by a property test over every reference, worked and control design.
Not: a built-in 2xx `accepted` for an HTTP write (the plan's criterion says what accepted is), nor a bigger budget.

### Q2. One model of what a design causes (the four QA contradiction rows are one principle)

`src/qa/backend/contradictions.mjs` holds four special-purpose rows -- MATCH_PINS/SECOND_SETTLED (77ed082),
NEVER_REDELIVERED (1aa7ba2), SETTLED_BEFORE (2d521bd), SELF_SATISFIED (9ecf4bb) -- for one principle: a verdict must
depend on the application, never on what the design itself causes, and never be one a correct application cannot meet.
Each row hard-codes operation ids and shape ids, and where refusal ends and advice begins was moved by hand twice after a
host ignored advice. Derive them from one model of what a design causes: the `effects` column of
`src/adapters/families.mjs`, the adapters' `means`/`selects`/semantics, and the shapes' declared semantics. Gaps a
review found (each a false alarm or a missed defect on a future model row):

- SECOND_SETTLED and SETTLED_BEFORE are gated on the shape id `effect-once-per-key`, while `shapes.mjs` states the same
  "a second one is not a second effect" rule for crash-after-effect-before-ack and competing-workers in prose only: a
  crash-after-effect design that waits for its redelivery to settle `processed` is neither refused nor advised (the
  ship-13 QA-001 false-alarm class). Make "what comes again" a declared semantics column of each shape; generate the
  `says` text and the refusal from it.
- NEVER_REDELIVERED reads the shape's `redeliveryTriggers`, while its own comment says the ADAPTER declares them
  (`adapters.json` nack, crash-before-settle, connection-loss, ack-wait); its cause test is any workload control
  anywhere (a setup `start`, a control after the wait, another workload all escape it); `delivery.attempt` is
  hard-coded where the job family says `execution.attempt`.
- MATCH_PINS applies only to `complete` waits: an `eq`/`in` assertion on a step whose answer-selecting match pins the
  same field to another value can never hold either (e.g. a scheduler observe pinned to `skipped` under a value-eq
  `executed`).
- SELF_SATISFIED covers messaging publish -> observe only: the same through `job.enqueue` or `scheduler.trigger` is a
  MISSED defect (a design that satisfies its own wait passes on a broken application), and its absent-within mirror (a
  design that triggers early, then rules absent-within) is a false alarm. Its refusal recommends "trigger its schedule"
  where UNCAUSED_DECISION says a trigger "proves the handler, never the schedule".
- UNCAUSED_DECISION (pre-check pattern 5) is still advice although it is decidable from the record (no clock step, the
  clock adapter declares injection, no earlier version that ran moved the clock); `recordOf`
  (`src/investigation/generate.mjs`) keeps only SATISFIED versions -- keep what every earlier version that RAN caused.
- After a SELF_SATISFIED refusal, a host that moves the clock to a period an earlier test already decided sees no new
  message (the agent that built it named this): the record of what earlier versions caused settles it too.

### Q6. The benchmark keeps what a person needs, not every scratch tree (disk)

During the 0.5.0 ship the cloud session's disk allowance (~39G) ran out twice: every control set's room keeps a
`scratch/` of ~390M (one every ~30s), and every room a frozen `checkout/` and `plugin/` (~85M) that nothing reads once
the run ended -- the ship and `--evidence` read only `manifest.json`, `ending.json`, `report.json`, `verified.json`

it needs and fail early, not mid-row.

### Q9. An investigation that never ran a test is not coverage

On that runner the QA judge credited every shape TESTED at "coverage 100%" while each one's runs ended
`EXECUTION_ERROR` or `AMBIGUOUS`: a run that came to no verdict was counted as a shape tested. Coverage (and TESTED)
must count only a shape whose runs reached SATISFIED or CONTRADICTED on the planted application; an execution error or
an ambiguous run is NOT_TESTED, said with why (benchmark/lib/qa/judge.mjs; held by benchmark-qa-judge tests). Check
whether any 0.5.0 ship row's coverage rested on such runs.
